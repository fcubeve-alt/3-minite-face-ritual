The camera

The AR Mirror feature uses your device's front camera to work out where your facial features are, so it can draw the movement path onto your own face.

  • The camera runs only while AR Mirror is open, and only after you grant permission.
  • Camera frames are analysed on your device, in real time, and then discarded. They are not recorded, not saved to disk, and not transmitted anywhere.
  • No photos or video of you are ever created.
  • You can withdraw camera access at any time in iOS Settings. Coach mode works without the camera.

What is stored on your device

The following is kept in the app's private storage on your device, and nowhere else:

  • Practice records — which routine you did, when, how long, and whether you finished it.
  • Settings — voice and haptics preferences, reminder times, preferred practice mode.

Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also clear your practice history at any time from Settings inside the app.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive or store your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No server is involved and no reminder data is sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • No health or medical data
  • No photos or video
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No tracking of your activity across other apps or websites

Information Apple provides

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymised statistics such as download counts and crash reports. We cannot identify you from this information. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All of your data lives on your device and is entirely under your control — you can remove it by clearing your history in Settings, or by deleting the app.

This also means that requests under regulations such as the GDPR or the CCPA have no data to act on. If you would like confirmation of that in writing, contact us at [[SUPPORT EMAIL]].
