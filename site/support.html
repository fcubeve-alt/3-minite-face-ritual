The AR Mirror can't find my face

A few things usually help: face a window or lamp rather than having the light behind you, hold the phone about an arm's length away, and keep your face roughly centred in the frame.

If it still doesn't lock on, the app will offer to switch to Coach mode — the timer keeps running and the session still counts. Nothing is lost.

I said no to the camera. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Coach mode never uses the camera and covers the whole routine. If you change your mind, you can grant access later in iOS Settings → face3 → Camera.

Does my camera video get uploaded?

No. Camera frames are analysed on your device and immediately discarded — nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy.

My hand covers my face during the movements

That's expected, and the app is built for it. When your hand blocks the view, the guidance holds its last known position and the routine keeps going. The app will never tell you that you did a movement "wrong" — it can't see what's under your hand, so it doesn't guess.

How do I restore a subscription on a new device?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions, then choose this app and cancel. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next renewal.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your device. Settings → About & Safety area has a Clear practice history option, and deleting the app removes everything else.

Is this medical advice?

No. face3 is a self-care routine guide. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything and makes no promises about how your face will look. Use a light touch, stop if anything hurts, and check with a qualified professional if you're unsure whether these movements suit you.
