Three minutes for your face,
first thing in the morning.

A short guided routine you do in front of your phone. It shows you where to put your hands and when to move on, so you can stop thinking and just do it.

Your camera stays on your device. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere. The app has no accounts, no tracking, and no third-party analytics. Read the privacy policy — it is short, and it describes what the code actually does.

How it works

  • 1Open the app and press start. There is nothing to set up.
  • 2Follow along either with an on-screen coach, or in mirror mode, where the app draws the path for each movement onto your own face.
  • 3It handles the timing and tells you when to switch sides. Three minutes later you are done.

Mirror mode uses the front camera to find the shape of your face, so the guide lands in the right place whether you are close or far, tilted or straight on. If it loses track — because your hand is covering your face, which happens constantly — the routine keeps going. It never stops to tell you that you are doing it wrong.

What this app does not do

It does not claim to change how you look. It does not promise to lift, tighten, slim, de-puff, or reduce anything.

It does not score you, grade your technique, or tell you that a movement was performed correctly or incorrectly. That judgement is not something a phone camera can honestly make, so the app does not pretend otherwise.

It is not a medical device and it is not a substitute for professional advice. If something hurts, stop.

The movements are drawn from published protocols and established hands-on practice, and they are reviewed by qualified practitioners before they ship. Appearing in a study is not the same as being individually proven to work, and we do not present it as such.

Who it is for

People who want a small, consistent thing to do in the morning. Three minutes is short enough that you will actually do it, and long enough to feel like something.

If you are looking for a product that promises visible results on a schedule, this is not that product, and we would rather tell you now.
